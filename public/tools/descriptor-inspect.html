Descriptors

Inspect descriptor

Parse a descriptor, compute its checksum, and try compiling or deriving its scriptPubKey.

How To Use It

Paste a descriptor. Optional branch and index fields are used for derivable descriptors. Taproot script leaves can use pk or multi_a.

Example

tr(0279be667ef9dcbbac55a06295ce870b07029bfcdb2dce28d959f2815b16f81798,multi_a(1,0279be667ef9dcbbac55a06295ce870b07029bfcdb2dce28d959f2815b16f81798,03f028892bad7ed57d2fb57bf33081d5cfcf6f9ed3d3d7f159c2e2fff579dc341a))
